Privacy Policy — Farsante

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Español

SensiaLabss (Spain) develops the app Farsante: Party Game Night (the "App"), a game for groups played on one phone (or two). This policy explains what data the App handles and how. Contact: sensialabss@gmail.com.

In short: everything you put into Farsante (player names, characters, photos and games) is stored only on your phone and never reaches us. We have no servers, there are no user accounts, no ads, and we use no analytics of our own and no advertising identifiers. The App does not ask for microphone or camera permission. The game works offline; the App only connects to the app store (purchases and ratings) and, if you use «Play with 2 phones», to the system code scanner (section 5).

1. Data you save and where it lives

What you enter is stored exclusively in the App's internal storage, on your phone:

  • The players' names or nicknames and the character each one chooses.
  • A player's photo, if you decide to add one (section 2).
  • The game in progress, your settings (sound, vibration, language), your achievements and the game statistics (for example, how many games you have finished).
  • When the App has shown you the rating window (date and number of times), so it is not shown to you too often; never your rating (section 6).
  • The receipt for your purchases (section 7).

This data is not sent to us or to third parties. We cannot see it. Nor is it included in the phone's automatic backups.

2. Player photos

Adding a photo is optional. If you do, you choose it with your phone's photo picker: the App does not ask for permission to use the camera or to read your files, and it only receives the photo you choose. The App crops it into a circle, shrinks it to a small size and saves it on your phone. The photo only leaves the phone if you share a mugshot that shows it (section 3).

If you add a photo of another person, do so with their permission. If you swap that player's face for a character, their photo is deleted from the phone when the next game starts. You can delete all photos and players with «Start over» in Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.

3. Sharing the mugshot

At the end of the game the App can draw, on your phone, a «mugshot» with a player's name and face (the character or the photo). It only leaves the phone if you tap «Share»: your phone's share menu then opens and the image goes to the app or person you choose, who handles it under their own rules. We do not receive it. To be able to share it, the App keeps a temporary copy in its internal memory, which is replaced each time you share another one. Before sharing another person's mugshot, ask for their permission.

4. Playing with two phones

With «Play with 2 phones», in each round the first phone shows a QR code with just what the other group needs: the deck and the card (by its number), the hint, and which player number in that group is the faker. The code contains no names or photos and expires with the round. The phones do not connect to each other or over the internet: the second phone reads the code with the camera and saves nothing.

5. The system code scanner

To read the QR code, the second phone uses the code scanner included in your phone's system services. The camera screen is provided by that scanner: the App does not ask for camera permission, does not see the image and only receives the text of the code.

When you use it, the provider of those system services may receive technical operating data: the phone's model and system version, the App's name and version, a technical identifier of the installation or of the scanning session (not intended to identify you or your phone) and performance and error measurements. It uses them to diagnose and improve the scanner, sends them encrypted and handles them under its own privacy policy. We do not receive them. If you do not want this to happen, play with a single phone.

6. Ratings

In Settings, «Rate us» opens Farsante's page in the app store, where you can leave your rating and your comment if you wish. In addition, from time to time, when a full game ends (never midway), the App may ask for a rating with the store's official window; that window does not always appear, because it is the store that decides whether to show it. Rating is voluntary and gives no advantage. Both the page and the window are shown and managed by the store, and the App does not receive your rating or your comment and does not know whether you have rated it.

What you write is published by the store under your account, according to its own rules; during testing phases, the store sends it to us privately. Like any store review, we can read it and reply to it in the store itself, and we use it only to improve Farsante. You can delete it from your store account.

7. Purchases

Farsante is free to download, with three modes and some decks included. The rest are unlocked with one-time payments (deck packs or «Everything»), with no subscription. Payment is handled by the app store you installed Farsante from, which acts as the seller and handles the purchase data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, your email or your payment details.

The store does provide us with sales reports showing the order number, product, date, amount and country of each purchase (and, in some cases, the province or postal code). We use them only to keep our accounts and meet our legal obligations, and we keep them for as long as those rules require. We store them in protected accounts, and only we have access.

To give you back what you bought if you change phones or reinstall, the App asks the store what your account has bought and saves on your phone the receipt the store provides (a purchase code), without sending it to us.

8. System permissions

  • Vibration: to alert you during your turn. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • In-store purchases: to buy decks or «Everything» and recover what you have already bought.
  • Internet access and network state: used by the store components (purchases and ratings) and by the system code scanner. The App never sends your players, names or photos.

The App does not ask for microphone, camera, location, contacts or files permission.

9. If you write to us

If you write to us at sensialabss@gmail.com, we will use your address and your message only to reply to you, and we will keep them while the enquiry lasts. We keep emails in a protected account, and only we have access.

10. Data we do NOT handle

  • There are no accounts, login or registration.
  • We do not use your location, your contacts, the microphone or the camera.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics of our own or ads.
  • We do not sell or pass on data to anyone.

11. Your rights

What the App saves lives on your phone: you can change or delete it from the App, or remove everything by uninstalling Farsante. For the little data that does reach us (sales reports, reviews and emails) you can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, objection or restriction (GDPR and the Spanish data protection act, LOPDGDD) by writing to sensialabss@gmail.com. You delete reviews yourself from your store account. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask for or collect data from any player.

13. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.
