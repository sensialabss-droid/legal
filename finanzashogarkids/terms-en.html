A personal, non-exclusive and non-transferable licence to use the App on the family's devices. 2. The App does NOT move real money

Flying Piggy Bank is a notebook and a game: it records what the child writes down and helps them think before spending. It is not a bank account, a card or a wallet. It does not connect to any bank, make payments, transfer funds or hold money. Real money is handled by the adult, outside the App. The figures in the App are the ones that have been written down and may not match the real money if not everything is recorded.

The App does not convert currencies. Amounts are shown in the currency that matches the phone's language and region (for example, euros, pounds, dollars or reais); if the language is changed, the currency symbol may change, but the number that was written down does not.

3. It is educational, not advice

The App's estimates (for example, how long it would take to reach a wish at the current pace) are approximate and come only from what has been written down. They are not financial advice of any kind.

4. The game, tokens, the map and the token shop

"The Savings Leap" is a game inside the App. Tokens are earned by playing and are only for use inside the App: to fill the piggy banks of the places on the journey map and to get things in the token shop (hats, trails, furniture for the house and the "Coin rain" mini-game). They are not money, they cannot be bought with real money, they cannot be converted into any currency and they cannot be sold or exchanged outside the App. When the game says "buy" in the token shop, it is paid only with tokens. What is obtained with tokens does not expire, and no purchase with money makes the character fly farther or win more easily.

The villains stand for spending temptations; they are beaten by answering questions about money decisions and with skill. Trophies, ranks, map treasures, album creatures and the companion's tricks are in-game rewards with no value outside it. The game has no energy, no loot boxes and no random prizes, and it can be played again whenever you like; the "Coin rain" mini-game has three lives per round and a daily cap on tokens. Nothing in the game is bought with money: no tokens, no lives and no advantages to win. The only paid part is Pro (section 5), which opens more worlds and characters (in those worlds tokens are also earned only by playing); it gives no advantage within a round.

5. Free version and Pro version

The free version includes: one child with their piggy bank, one wish, two starting characters and two more earned with saving achievements, "Can I buy it?", "Let me think", the default jar split, up to three active chores per child, allowance given by hand, the game (with the first journey's map, the first world of the second adventure, the token shop, the album and the mini-game) and the backup.

The Pro version (one-time payment, only from the parents' area) adds: several children on the same phone, each with their own piggy bank; unlimited wishes; four more characters; the whole second adventure: three more worlds, with their neighbours, stories and villains; changing the jar split; unlimited chores; automatic allowance (every week, every two weeks or every month); how many days a purchase delays the next wish; and the question "Was it worth it?" with its decision stats.

What already exists is never locked: chores that have been created can always still be seen, ticked, approved and deleted, and an automatic allowance that is already switched on keeps working even if the Pro purchase cannot be checked at that moment. Families who were already using chores or the automatic allowance before these limits existed keep them without Pro.
