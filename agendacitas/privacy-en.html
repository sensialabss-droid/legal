Privacy Policy — Agenda de Citas

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Español

SensiaLabss (Spain) develops Agenda de Citas (the "App"), an appointment book for self-employed professionals (nails, lashes, brows, barbering and similar). This policy explains what data the App handles and how. Contact: sensialabss@gmail.com.

In short: the App is local-first. Your appointments, your clients, your services, your payments and the photos you add are stored only on your device. There are no servers holding your data, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. We cannot see your data or your clients' data. It only leaves your phone when you decide: when you send a WhatsApp message or a text message (SMS) to a client, when you add an appointment to your calendar, or when you share your backup.

1. Who is responsible for what

  • You are responsible for your clients' data, as the professional who records it and decides what it is used for. The App is a tool that runs on your phone: we do not receive, see or store that data.
  • We only handle the little data that reaches us from purchases (section 5) and whatever you tell us if you email us.

Inside the App, under Settings → «Privacy and your clients», you will find a short guide with tips and a text you can use to inform your clients.

2. Data you save and where it lives

It is stored exclusively in the App's private storage on your device:

  • Your business: the name you type, your opening hours and your settings.
  • Your services, with their length and price.
  • Your clients: name, phone number and any notes you write. You can also copy the name and number from a contact on your phone (section 4).
  • Your appointments: day, time, service, price, status (pending, paid, cancelled, no-show), notes and whether you sent the reminder.
  • The days or hours you are not available, with a reason if you choose to add one (for example, «Holidays» or «Doctor»). The reason is just for you: it is not included in the messages to your clients.
  • Your payments, only as a record (cash, card, bank transfer or other). The App does not charge anyone, does not connect to banks or to any payment method and does not issue invoices or receipts.
  • With full access, photos of your work that you add to a client's card. The App keeps a reduced copy; the original photo stays where it was (for example, in your gallery).

None of this is sent to us or to third parties. We do not ask for an account, registration, name or email address. Your appointment book is not included in the system's cloud backup or in direct phone-to-phone transfers (including to an iPhone): to change phones, use the App's own backup.

Health notes. The App has no health field and recommends that you do not record health data about your clients (for example, allergies). If you decide to write any in the notes, do so only with your client's permission: you are responsible for that data.

3. When data leaves your phone (only if you ask)

  • WhatsApp. When you send a reminder or a confirmation, tap the WhatsApp button, or let a client know that you are moving their appointment or cannot see them, the App opens WhatsApp with the client's number and the message already written (their first name, the day, the time, the service and, if you use it, your business name). You send the message yourself from your own WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. If WhatsApp does not open directly, the link opens in the browser, on the WhatsApp website. «Remind everyone» opens the messages one after another: you send each one yourself.
  • Text message (SMS). If you choose in Settings to send messages by SMS instead of WhatsApp, those same messages (reminders, confirmations and notices) open in your phone's messaging app, with your client's number and the text already written. You send it yourself; it goes through your mobile carrier and may cost whatever your plan charges (texts to numbers in other countries may cost more). The App does not send texts on its own and does not use any server of ours.
  • Call. Opens your phone's dialler with the client's number.
  • Add to my calendar. On a pending appointment, this button opens your phone's calendar app with a new event already filled in: the day and times of the appointment and, as the title, the service and the client's name (for example, «Nails · Rocío»). It does not include their phone number, notes or the price. The event is only saved if you confirm it in your calendar. From then on it is handled by your calendar app, under its own terms: if your calendar is synced with an online account, the event is also stored in that account, and anyone with access to that calendar (for example, if you share it) can see it. The App cannot change or delete that event: if you change or delete the appointment, or delete the client, you will need to change or delete it yourself in the calendar.
  • Backup. When you ask for it, the App creates a .json file with your whole appointment book: appointments, clients with their notes, services, settings (including the days you are not available and their reasons) and photos. The file is not password-protected: anyone who has it can read it. You share it wherever you choose with your phone's share menu, and it only goes where you send it; we do not receive it. To restore a backup, you pick the file and the App reads only that one.
  • Our other apps and the App's store page. These open the app store. The link carries a tag showing which app you came from, which the store reads; it contains no data from your appointment book.

4. System permissions

  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily alert («You have N appointments tomorrow. The first one is at …»). The alert does not include any client's name. Depending on your phone's settings, it may be visible on the lock screen. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Photos: the App uses the system photo picker and only receives the photo you choose. It does not ask for permission to read your gallery and does not use the camera directly.
  • Contacts: the App does not ask for permission to see your contacts. If you tap «Choose from my contacts», your phone opens its own list and the App only receives the name and number of the contact you choose, which go into the appointment or client card you are filling in. It does not see your other contacts or change them.
  • Calendar: the App does not ask for permission to read or change your calendar. It only opens your calendar app with the event filled in (section 3); it does not see your other events.
  • Text messages: the App does not ask for permission to send or read text messages. It only opens your messaging app, and you are the one who sends.
  • Files you export or open: the backup, when you ask for it.
  • Internet connection: used by the app store for purchases and to tell you when a new version is available, and by WhatsApp, the browser or your calendar app when you open them. The App does not use it to send your appointment data. The appointment book works offline.

5. Purchases

The App is free to download and has a full access option unlocked with a one-time payment, with no subscription. Payment is handled by the app store you installed the App from, which shows you the final price and, in many countries (including the European Union and the United Kingdom), acts as the seller. We do not see or store your payment details. Like any developer, we receive from the store the order number and country of each purchase, never your name or your bank details. We use this only to keep our accounts and meet our legal obligations, and we keep it for as long as those obligations require. To give you back full access if you change phones or reinstall, the App asks the store whether your account has already bought it and saves on your phone the receipt the store provides, without sending it to us.

The store's purchase library sends the store technical information about how it works. It does not carry any data from your appointment book: neither your appointments nor your clients' data.

6. Data we do NOT handle

  • There are no accounts, logins or email addresses needed to use the App.
  • We do not collect your location or advertising identifiers, and we do not see your contact list or your calendar: only the contact you choose (section 4).
  • We do not use analytics or ads, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.
  • The App does not track your activity across other apps or websites, so it does not need to respond to «Do Not Track» signals.

7. Your rights and your clients' rights

You are in control: you can edit or delete any data from the App, delete everything with «Delete all my data» or uninstall the App. You can take out all your data, free of charge and whenever you like, with «Back up now» in Settings (a .json file you can move to another phone with «Restore a backup»). Anything you saved to your calendar is deleted from your calendar.

Your clients: since we do not have their data, they must ask you if they want to see, correct or delete it. You can delete a client from their card: the card and its photos are deleted; their past appointments stay in your book with their name only. Copies you have saved outside the phone and events you added to your calendar are not deleted automatically.

For the little data that reaches us (purchase reports and emails), you can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, objection or restriction under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish data protection act (LOPDGDD), by writing to sensialabss@gmail.com. If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

8. Children

The App is a work tool for professionals and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.
